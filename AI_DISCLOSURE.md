# AI Assistance Disclosure

## Project

**Think Without Words — Recurrent Latent-Space Reasoning**

## AI-assisted work

AI assistance was used during development for:

- brainstorming and narrowing the educational concept;
- drafting and pressure-testing the falsifiable claim;
- proposing and revising the synthetic experiment;
- debugging Python, Streamlit, PyTorch, and import/runtime issues;
- drafting portions of application code;
- drafting README and documentation text;
- suggesting visualization and interaction patterns.

## Human responsibility

The project author is responsible for:

- selecting the final concept;
- reviewing and testing the implementation;
- running the experiments;
- inspecting the measured results;
- deciding which claims are supported by the experiment;
- verifying research sources;
- understanding the architecture and code;
- correcting implementation issues;
- preparing and defending the final submission.

## Important distinction

AI-generated or AI-assisted material is not treated as evidence.

Research claims about latent reasoning, recurrent-depth computation, BDH, and BDH-CQ are grounded in the cited primary sources. The toy simulator is explicitly identified as an educational abstraction.
